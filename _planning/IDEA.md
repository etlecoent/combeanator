# Home Page Ideas for Combeanator

## Overview
Combeanator is a coffee bean aggregator that helps users search, compare, and discover coffee beans from multiple retailers/roasters in one place.

## Home Page Sections

### 1. Hero Section
**Goal**: Immediately communicate value and enable search

- **Headline**: "Find Your Perfect Brew" or "Compare Coffee Beans Across Retailers"
- **Subheadline**: Brief value prop - "Search, compare, and discover coffee beans from multiple roasters in one place"
- **Primary CTA**: Search bar to find beans by:
  - Bean name
  - Origin (country/region)
  - Roast level
  - Flavor notes
- **Hero Visual**: Coffee bean imagery or animated illustration

### 2. Key Features Section
**Goal**: Highlight core value propositions

- **Price Comparison**: Find the best deals across retailers
- **Flavor Profiles**: Filter by tasting notes (chocolate, fruity, nutty, floral, etc.)
- **Roast Levels**: Light, medium, dark filters
- **Origin Discovery**: Explore beans by country/region (Ethiopia, Colombia, Brazil, etc.)

### 3. Quick Search/Browse
**Goal**: Enable immediate exploration

Options:
- Popular origins (Ethiopia, Colombia, Brazil, Kenya, Guatemala, etc.)
- Trending beans or new arrivals
- Featured roasters/retailers
- Curated collections (e.g., "Fruity & Bright", "Bold & Chocolatey")

### 4. Social Proof
**Goal**: Build trust and show scale

Display:
- Number of beans indexed (e.g., "10,000+ beans tracked")
- Number of retailers/roasters tracked (e.g., "50+ roasters")
- User testimonials or ratings (if applicable)
- Trust indicators

### 5. How It Works
**Goal**: Simple explanation for new users

Three-step process:
1. **Search or browse** beans by origin, flavor, or roast
2. **Compare** prices, ratings, and reviews across retailers
3. **Buy** from your preferred retailer with one click

### 6. Call to Action
**Goal**: Drive engagement

- Sign up for price alerts
- Browse featured beans
- Subscribe to newsletter for new bean discoveries

---

## Design Considerations

- Maintain coffee-themed branding (espresso, cream, tan, amber colors)
- Keep it clean and scannable
- Make search prominent and easy to use
- Mobile-responsive design
- Fast loading with lazy-loaded images
